**First comment:**
I made a small daily football game. You get five free kicks a day and everyone gets the same
five. You swipe from the ball to draw its path: curve the swipe to bend it round the wall,
swipe faster to hit it harder (harder is less accurate). The keeper reads slow shots, so you
need pace and placement. No sign-up, no ads, works on phone and desktop, saves on your device.
There is a practice mode if you burn through today's five.

I would like to know two things: does the swipe feel right on your phone, and is the keeper
too good or too easy?
